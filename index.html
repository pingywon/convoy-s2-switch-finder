Will a new tail switch fit your Convoy S2+?

Not every S2+ can take one. About half the finishes ship with a pressure-fit tail that cannot be removed without damage — and the split doesn't follow price or material the way you'd expect. Pick your finish and this tells you in one step.

What this guide does not cover

  • Ti Purple Swirl is absent from the manufacturer's compatibility list entirely. We have not verified it either way, so it is marked undocumented rather than guessed at — ask us before ordering a switch for one.
  • We have no published explanation for why the pressure-fit hosts differ, only that they do.
  • Whether fitting the 18350 short tube changes any of this is undocumented.
